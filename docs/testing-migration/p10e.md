Behavior 09, fifth slice: every `cp_decide` rule a parent can reach, through a real pi parent, the command-post
extension and a mock model. Checkpoints, grants, escalations and Awaiting rows are seeded on disk; the operator quotes
are the session's own user messages. Each control was applied alone to `src/`, failed on the named assertion, and
passed once restored. `tests/decision-context.test.ts` and `tests/questions.test.ts` stay in place as retained files
(evidence below); `tests/answered.test.ts` and `tests/awaiting.test.ts` stay `convert` / P10.

Retained in place, no entry point:

- `tests/decision-context.test.ts`: `CommandPost.decisionContext` (`src/command-post.ts`) is the only caller of
  `buildDecisionContext` and `readDecisionEvidence`, and nothing calls it; no tool, command, widget or RPC event
  renders the decision pane, so neither the pane nor its redaction and bounds have a consumer to drive.
- `tests/questions.test.ts`: `QuestionRelay.handle` (and the policy, journal writer and review-dialog shaping behind
  it) runs only when a planner worker opens a dialog. None does: `ask_operator` needs `WorkerIdentity.mayAskOperator`,
  which nothing sets since cur.3.1, and no other worker extension calls `ctx.ui`. `authorizationTrigger`'s units pass
  custom patterns no caller passes; its default-pattern lint on Awaiting rows is pinned by P10c-rows.
