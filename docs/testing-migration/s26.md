## Product defect (fixed by cp-fix-restart-kills-rpc-pi-fky9)

Until that fix, an accepted dashboard restart left the old RPC pi and its serving socket alive: under `--mode rpc`,
pi's `ctx.shutdown()` only flags a shutdown that runs on the next stdin command or settled turn, and a socket-driven
restart is neither. `relaunchPorts().shutdown` now follows `ctx.shutdown()` with pi's own SIGTERM handler under rpc.
S26-dashboard asserts the pid exits and the socket stops serving unconditionally; the two former pending-fix
