## Parked product failure

Dashboard restart declarations 3 and 4 include real shutdown. With a persisted real RPC bridge, restart returns
`restarting` and writes the marker, but `isPidAlive(marker.pid)` remains true after 30 seconds and the control socket
remains live. This was reproduced three times, including a direct owned-PID assertion. Those declarations remain
pending-fix in their original file; the passing marker-only scenario does not claim shutdown coverage.
