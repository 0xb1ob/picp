Behavior 18, second slice. P18-parked (on main from P18a) now drives the whole parked lifecycle through the landing
stack (`tests/e2e/landing-helpers.ts`) and reads the home through `bin/cp-view`: the park through `cp_integrate`, its
refusals of teardown, revive and re-dispatch, a team review seen by `awaitingMerge.sweep`, a real pi parent's
`cp_status_block` and `cp_next`, refused and accepted `cp_send` resumes onto a teammate's pushed tip, a second park,
the moved head's CI, and the human merge with its after-merge line. The pages are read as DOM, text and HTTP, never
pixels. P18-parked-edges drives the handoff decision and the watch into their faults by real means: a garbage
`data/projects.json` or `fleet.json` and mislabelled records on disk, an unreadable mergeStateStatus, a dirty worktree,
GitHub's path-less errors, exit codes and rate limits through the gh fake, and two sweeps held on one gh call. Each
retained row carries a V2 line: the function it calls and the injected fault no real surface produces.

The UI files map onto served tabs: `viewer-jobs-qa` onto P18-lanes and P18-jobs-ui (on main), `viewer-mandates-map`
onto P18-map-api, `viewer-app-navigation` onto P18-nav (every hash's data read and lit nav item on both navs), and
`viewer-job-transcript-render` onto P18-transcript-ui; its layout and poll-timing helpers are retained.
