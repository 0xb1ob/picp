The default status order retains the original phone order: Held, Working, Waiting, Launching, Queued, Failed, Landed today. Filled columns precede folded strips. Fixtures intentionally contain different jobs and counts from the artboards.

At 390 px, the page width is 390 px and the columns/strips are 475 px tall; their horizontal region scrolls 744 px. A Chromium touch gesture moves the region, and the tall Working column scrolls 112 px internally. At 1440 px, the page width is 1440 px and columns/strips are 665 px tall. Long titles, objectives and escalation notes stay within the layout; full titles/objectives remain in tooltips. Mandate filters retain paused work and restore every job when All mandates is selected. Browser traffic allowed GET only, with zero attempted mutations.

The supplied compare.cjs was adapted for this leased worktree: local import/output paths, loopback-only base, a deterministic Board GET fixture, design privacy masks, and a Sessions readiness check compatible with unavailable composer controls. Its original 25-artboard manifest, dimension checks, credential redaction and GET-only routing were retained. All 25 before and after pairs have matching dimensions. Only the Board sheets are published here.
