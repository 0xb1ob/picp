- Durable wake-ups no longer vanish when another process enqueues one while the parent rewrites `state/wakeups.json`: every outbox read-modify-write now runs under a short cross-process `wakeups.json.lock` (O_EXCL, stale after 10 s), and a failed sweep or arrival confirm is written to stderr instead of swallowed. Test waits cap their `last =` detail at 4,000 chars so a timeout's trail stays in `gh run view --log` (a 470 KB line used to cut the step log at the failing test).
