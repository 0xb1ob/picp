- Quota continuation hardening (P3 PR3, still disabled in production): an unref'd due timer sends a pending intent's continuation after its reset (armed on claim and at startup, stopped on shutdown); a due intent waits for a free worker slot and for a drain to clear, and cancels when its job closed, its mandate refuses a repair (revoked, capped or past an explicit deadline), or the worker was revived by hand. A continuation resumes the interrupted round's tool-call count and wall clock instead of renewing them; a parent that stops mid-delivery writes nothing further, so the next owner holds the intent as ambiguous and never resends it. A pending intent keeps bounded recovery and startup reconcile from treating its job as ordinarily revivable, and a store that turns unreadable mid-pass is journaled per job instead of throwing out of the tick.
