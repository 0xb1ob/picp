- Known CI flakes (cp-a0gs): `cp_integrate` reruns a red first attempt once, without spending the repair, when every failed job is a listed gate or every entry of its `✖ failing tests:` block is in `defaults/ci-flakes.json` for the project, its test-suite step is its only failed step and the block matches the reporter's `ℹ fail N` (read from that attempt's `gh run view --attempt <n> --log-failed`; an unreadable or miscounted log refuses). Known-flake claims gain optional `attempt`/`outcome`: an `uncertain` claim left by a crash is settled from the run's attempts and never reruns, and a stale red read of an accepted flake rerun waits. Infra-only reruns (PR and main CI) are unchanged. The CI watch re-sends an unconfirmed `cp-ci` fact once after the retry interval, then at most every 10 minutes until it arrives or is no longer derived, instead of going silent after one resend.
