- P3 PR4 quota continuation, operator surface: `/cp-quota` and `cp_parent quota` (status, on/off, cancel) report **unsupported in production** (capability `none`) first; a policy alone schedules nothing. The Job page shows the job's effective policy and latest intent (overdue, held, local reset time with UTC) and offers cancel only through the operator. A hold or command-post cancel queues one distinct `quota:` wake. An older parent refuses `cp_parent quota` as unregistered. Intents now record `worker_started_at` beside an optional `worker_pid`, so a reused pid is not mistaken for the interrupted worker and older intents still load.
