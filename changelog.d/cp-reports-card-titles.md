- Reports: card titles in Published and Job reports stay inside their cards at every width. Report cards have their own `report-card*` classes instead of the Jobs table's row classes, so a long question or bare URL wraps, clamps to two lines with an ellipsis (full title in a tooltip), and the date keeps its own column on the heading row. On a phone the page title "Reports" is one line again, with the intro under it at full width. The Jobs page is unchanged. `tests/e2e/viewer-reports-card-titles.test.ts` checks the geometry in headless Chrome.
