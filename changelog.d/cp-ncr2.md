- Boards can be served on their own port, apart from the dashboard: an opt-in boards-only listener (`data/daemon.json` `boards_port`/`boards_host`/`boards_index`, or `CP_BOARDS_PORT`/`CP_BOARDS_HOST`/`CP_BOARDS_INDEX`) answers only `/boards/board.css` and `/boards/<slug>/…` and 404s every dashboard route; `data/viewer.json` `boards_public_url` points board links at it, and `/doctor` prints a `[boards]` line. See docs/service.md §Boards on their own port.
