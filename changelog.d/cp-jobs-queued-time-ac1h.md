- Jobs page: every queued or waiting job shows when it was queued (and which stamp that is), a live "waiting" duration and one server-derived reason — blocked by jobs, a full CLI slot (n/max), a full grant or run slot, no grant, a paused or expired grant, backlog, a schedule anchor; a started job's detail keeps "waited". `cp_next` and `cp_status_block` carry the same reason and wait. Dispatch-queue entries record the CLI that parked them (`waiting_driver`) and a started entry's `queued_at` stays on its fleet record.
