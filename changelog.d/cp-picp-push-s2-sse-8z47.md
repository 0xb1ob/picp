- Dashboard push refresh, slice 2: the viewer's `/api/stream` stops its unconditional 2 s `refresh` and pushes by change. It stats each view's sources once a second while a stream is open, and sends `data: {"views":[…]}` naming only the changed views (plus every view on a 30 s clock), with a `: hb` heartbeat at idle. `?views=a,b` sits beside the legacy `?view=a`. A live read turns stale after 75 s instead of 5 s. The viewer restarts on the normal update path; a tab opened before the update keeps updating on change but shows "stale" at idle until it is reloaded.
